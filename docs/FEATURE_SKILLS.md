# Skills & Two-Encounter Prototype — decisions

- One skill draft is shown before encounter 1; one after winning encounter 1.
- Drafts present up to three **unowned** skills, chosen randomly without replacement. Three initially, two on second draft. Empty pool advances automatically.
- Battle pauses completely while the draft is visible. One tap chooses exactly one skill.
- Owned skill bar always shows selected skills, including between encounters.
- After encounter 1, the hero's **current HP persists**; stats and skills persist; encounter counters and Final Stand uses reset.
- Fireball: on each hero basic attack against a living target, independently 50% chance to deal an **additional** magic hit equal to 150% of that attack's unmodified roll. No bonus critical.
- Iron Skin: on physical hits against the hero, subtract armor first, then reduce by 20%, rounded to nearest integer, with at least 1 damage. Magic bypasses both armor and Iron Skin.
- Final Stand: **once per encounter** lethal damage leaves the hero at 1 HP, negates the excess damage and prevents any additional hits within that same action. Immunity expires before the next character acts.
- Bone Alchemist: every third **skeleton action** replaces its basic hit with a fire potion equal to 100% of its rolled ATK; this counts as magic damage. Counter resets at encounter start.
- Loss ends the run, with replay available; replay resets skills, HP, and both encounters.
- Visuals remain placeholder art, no external artwork required.
