# Prototype decisions

- 2D side-view, portrait, approachable chibi-styled fantasy.
- One hero vs one enemy in the initial proof of concept.
- Fully automatic alternating turns. No action input during combat.
- Damage variability and occasional critical hits.
- Replay button for repeated fights.
- Combat model independent from the Godot rendering and UI.
- Later: skill-choice cards pause progress; randomized offerings and synergies.
- Later still: stages, loot, equipment, status effects, bosses, and optional auto-farming.
- Automation, monetization, multiplayer and persistent saves are not MVP features.
- Original game, not reproduction of third-party art/assets or branded characters.
